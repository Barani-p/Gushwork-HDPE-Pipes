Advanced HDPE Pipe Manufacturing Process

Our state-of-the-art extrusion technology ensures consistent quality, optimal material properties, and dimensional accuracy in every pipe we manufacture.

High-Grade Raw Material Selection

Vacuum sizing tanks ensure precise outer diameter while internal pressure maintains perfect roundness and wall thickness uniformity.

  • PE100 grade material
  • Optimal molecular weight distribution

Precision Extrusion Technology

Advanced twin-screw extruders melt and homogenize PE100 resin under precisely controlled temperature profiles for consistent melt quality.

  • Temperature-controlled extrusion zones
  • Consistent melt pressure & flow rate

Controlled Cooling Process

Water-cooled tanks maintain optimal cooling rates to lock in material properties and ensure dimensional stability across the full pipe length.

  • Uniform radial cooling
  • Residual stress minimization

Precision Vacuum Sizing

Vacuum sizing tanks ensure the exact outer diameter is maintained while internal pressure guarantees perfect wall thickness and roundness.

  • ±0.1 mm dimensional tolerance
  • Automated diameter monitoring

Rigorous Quality Control

Every pipe batch undergoes hydrostatic pressure testing, dimensional checks, and material property validation to meet IS 5984, ISO 4427 & ASTM D3035 standards.

  • 100% hydrostatic pressure test
  • BIS, ISO & ASTM certification

Permanent Pipe Marking

Ink-jet marking systems print permanent identification including brand, size, pressure rating, SDR, batch number and manufacturing date along the pipe length.

  • UV-resistant permanent ink
  • Full traceability per batch

Precision Automated Cutting

Automated planetary saw cutters deliver precise, burr-free cuts to required lengths — from standard 6 m / 12 m sticks to custom lengths up to 20 m.

  • ±5 mm length accuracy
  • Burr-free square end cuts

Protective Packaging & Dispatch

Pipes are bundled, capped, and strapped on wooden pallets or coiled on reels for coils — ensuring zero damage during transportation across India and export markets.

  • End-cap & UV wrap protection
  • Pan-India & export-ready dispatch
